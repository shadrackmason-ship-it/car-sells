# Actual vehicle photos

The current inventory is intentionally empty. No sample vehicles, estimated vehicle prices, or unrelated stock photos are presented as Kilimani Garage stock.

When adding a vehicle to `data/cars.js`, add its genuine photo inside this `images/` folder and set that record's `img` path to the matching filename, for example `images/toyota-prado-tx.jpg`. Use photos of that exact vehicle or a clearly approved representative image. Do not use a Pexels search result as proof of the exact vehicle for sale.

Optional detail-gallery files can use the same basename with `-2.jpg` and `-3.jpg`, for example `toyota-prado-tx-2.jpg` and `toyota-prado-tx-3.jpg`.

General site photo slots remain `hero.jpg`, `workshop.jpg`, `team.jpg`, and `gallery-1.jpg` through `gallery-5.jpg`.
